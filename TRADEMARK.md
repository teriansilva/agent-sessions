# Trademark policy

**BattleLab** — the name, the wordmark, the logo, and the visual identity — is a trademark of
Marcus Braun. The code is licensed [AGPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE); **the trademark is not.**

This isn't a restriction the AGPL added — AGPL-3.0 grants no trademark rights in the first place
(section 7(e) explicitly allows a licensor to decline them). This file just says out loud what the
license already implies, so nobody has to guess.

## What you can do without asking

- **Use BattleLab**, self-host it, modify it, and redistribute it under the AGPL.
- **Say your project works with BattleLab**, integrates with it, or is built on it — nominative use
  ("a plugin for BattleLab", "compatible with BattleLab") is fine and always has been.
- **Name BattleLab** in documentation, comparisons, reviews, talks, blog posts, and academic work.
- **Keep the branding on an unmodified build** you host for yourself or your own organisation.
  Running stock BattleLab under its own name is exactly the intended use.

## What needs a different name

- **Distributing a modified BattleLab under the BattleLab name.** Fork all you like — the AGPL
  guarantees that — but ship it as *your* thing. A fork carrying the original branding makes your
  bugs look like ours and our releases look like yours, which is precisely the confusion trademark
  law exists to prevent.
- **Offering a hosted or commercial service called "BattleLab"** (or a name close enough to be
  confusable), or otherwise presenting yourself as official, affiliated, or endorsed.
- **Using the logo as your own** project's or product's mark.

Renaming a fork means the name, the wordmark, and the logo. You do **not** have to strip
attribution — please keep the copyright notices, and a line like "based on BattleLab" is welcome
and accurate.

## Questions

If you're unsure whether a use is fine, ask before shipping — the answer is usually yes, and it's
much cheaper than a rename later. Enquiries and permission requests:
[battlelab.superstatus.io](https://battlelab.superstatus.io).
